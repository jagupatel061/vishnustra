Months named by stars

Every Indian lunar month takes its name from the nakshatra the full Moon sits in: Chaitra from Chitra, Kartika from Krittika, Shravana from Shravana. Here are the next twelve full moons, and where the Moon really is that night.

How the dates are found: Moon position by Jean Meeus's formulas, Lahiri ayanamsa, Indian Standard Time. Star festivals take the Moon's star at sunrise in the right solar month; full-moon festivals take the full-moon day of the right lunar month. A local panchang may differ by one day. A dashed month is adhika: an extra leap month, added when two new moons fall in the same solar month.
