The Sky as Vishnu

The Vishnu Purana pictures the whole night sky as Shishumara, the celestial porpoise, the star-form of Vishnu, with Dhruva the pole star at its tail. Tap any of the 27 stars on its body to read its shloka.

Stars are placed in order along the body for this illustration.
